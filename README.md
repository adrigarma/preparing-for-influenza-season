# Preparing for Influenza Season
Every year, flu season puts hospitals under immense pressure. Vulnerable populations, especially the elderly, are at higher risk,  often developing serious complications that require hospitalization. As hospital beds fill up, the demand for additional medical staff becomes critical. 

## Objective
The objective of this project is to perform a **predictive analysis** to support medical staffing decisions during influenza season across the United States.

## Key Questions
- How does the size of the elderly population impact influenza-related mortality risk?
- Where is the elderly population most concentrated?
- Which regions report the highest number of influenza-related deaths?
- When will additional staffing be most needed?

## Data
The analysis combined two main datasets:
- **Influenza deaths by geography**, sourced from the _CDC (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention)._
- **Population data by geography, time, age, and gender**, sourced from the _US Census Bureau_. 

## Tools
The project was developed using: 
- **Excel** for data cleaning, integration, transformation, and statistical analysis (including hypothesis testing).
- **Tableau** for building an interactive storyboard that enables data exploration and visual storytelling.
